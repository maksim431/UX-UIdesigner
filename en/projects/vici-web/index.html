  1. Task

After a rebrand, build a new website: minimalist, easy to use on any device, and one that makes people stay longer and then buy the products in a store.

  1. Solution

Research

Why people visit the site

Not for the specs of herring, but for an answer to “what should I cook for dinner”. So we need to sell not the product but a delicious dish.

  1. Where shoppers got lost

    On the old site the catalog and recipes weren’t connected. A person would open the catalog, hit a dead end and leave.

AI went through customer reviews, popular search queries and the old site structure in hours instead of days.

Hypothesis

Let’s turn the site from a dry catalog into a cooking magazine and connect recipes with specific products. Then people will stay on the site longer and go to the store with a ready shopping list.

What I did

  1. Connected products and recipes. A product page has a “What to cook with it” block, and a recipe has “Ingredients from VICI” with a link to the product. People move from inspiration to purchase and back. It also helps the site rank in search engines.

  1. Similar recipes. Below a recipe the site suggests similar dishes — for example, other quick breakfasts after shrimp croissants.

AI quickly matched recipes with the brand’s products. I saved hours of manual work and showed the client a prototype with real content.

  1. Tags like “Quick”, “For breakfast”, “Seafood”. With them, the right recipe is just a couple of taps away.

AI tagged the whole recipe database with these labels.

  1. A mobile version for the shopper at the shelf. Large buttons, the menu always at hand, recipes easy to scroll with one hand.

AI built the basic mobile grids, and I spent the time saved on details that affect usability.

  1. Handed developers a clean file with a design system, so the site was built quickly.

An AI agent did the routine file prep; I only reviewed it.

  1. Result

More pages per visit: people move from products to recipes and back.

More purchases in stores: people leave the site with a dinner plan and know which pack to look for.

The site became a “cookbook in your pocket” that people want to come back to.

The brand got younger: modern design and beautiful food attracted a younger audience.
