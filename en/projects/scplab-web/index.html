sCPlab

SCP LAB is a mobile coaching app for professional athletes. It builds a training program, shows video lessons from pros and tracks progress.

in this case

How to get +10% more paid subscriptions?

Making a phone easy to use right in the gym

What makes users come back 20% more often?

The “I can do it myself with AI” section

  1. Task

The client came with a clear goal: more people should buy a paid subscription after the free trial, and starting a workout should be quick and easy. A separate request was a premium “glass” style.

  1. Solution

Research

I studied competitor apps: what works for them and what users complain about.

The path to payment. Apps usually ask for money right at launch or after a boring questionnaire. People leave without understanding what they would pay for.

Where the app is used. In the gym, with sweaty hands, out of breath, with glare on the screen. Small buttons and long text don’t work there.

What brings people back. A professional already knows the basic technique — they need video lessons from top coaches.

In a couple of minutes, AI went through hundreds of reviews of competitor apps on the App Store and Google Play and pulled out the main complaints. Doing it by hand would have taken a day, and the client saved budget.

Hypothesis

People are more willing to pay for something they’ve already tried. If an athlete first takes a free test with video lessons and invests their own effort into the app, the personal program becomes valuable to them. Then paying is the logical next step.

What I did

  1. Moved payment to the end. First the person takes a free test with video lessons. Then the app reports that their personal program is ready, and only then offers a subscription.

AI helped me quickly build and check this journey and find the best moment to offer payment.

  1. Made video the star of the workout screen. A large player, a timer and a couple of buttons — nothing else.

The real videos hadn’t been shot yet, so I generated similar footage with AI. That way I checked in advance that text and buttons are readable on top of the video, and the client didn’t have to pay for rework after the shoot.

  1. Made buttons large and high-contrast. They are easy to hit even in the middle of a set.

AI plugins in Figma showed where users look and helped choose button sizes without long manual testing.

  1. Style. A deep black background with a bright neon-yellow accent is easy to read in any gym lighting. I used “glass” in cards and pop-ups: it looks premium and doesn’t distract from the video.

  1. Handed developers a clean file. It’s a ready design system with all screens and button states. Development goes faster and without extra questions, which means it costs the client less.

An AI agent in Figma did the routine file prep (layer names, styles, component checks) many times faster than by hand.

  1. Result

+10% subscriptions: more people buy the full subscription after the trial, because they pay for a program they’ve already tried.

30% faster from launching the app to starting a workout.

+20% returns: users come back to the app more often — it’s convenient to use right in the gym.
