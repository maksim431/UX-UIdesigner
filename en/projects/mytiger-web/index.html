my tiger

“My Tiger” is a premium pet clinic in Moscow: veterinary care and grooming. I built a separate landing page for it that brings in booking requests.

in this case

How to show that grooming is medicine, not just a haircut?

How to run a promotion without looking cheaper?

How to make booking short from anywhere on the page

The “I can do it myself with AI” section

  1. Task

The client needed a landing page that brings in requests — in addition to the main website. The wishes: brand patterns with tiger silhouettes and claw marks, real photos, a premium style and unusual solutions. And most importantly — to show that grooming here isn’t just about looks but a medical procedure done by professionals.

  1. Solution

Research

Who the clients are. Owners for whom the pet is part of the family. They choose a clinic by trust, not by price: who will treat and groom their pet and how animals are treated here.

How grooming is usually sold. As “wash and trim” — with competition on price. But the owner wants the groomer to notice a skin or ear problem and show the pet to a vet.

Why a simple discount won’t do. “−30%” and crossed-out prices look cheap in a premium clinic and scare away exactly the clients it’s all for.

Hypothesis

If we show the clinic through its people and approach, and present special offers as care rather than discounts, there will be more requests and the brand won’t lose its status.

What I did

  1. A premium style. Deep green, milky white and chocolate, like the clinic’s interiors. Tiger silhouettes and claw marks on the dark side panel and in the details. Real photos of pets and specialists instead of stock images.

  1. An unusual layout. A dark panel with the pattern and the clinic’s special offer is always visible on the left, everything else is on the right. Booking opens on top of the page over a photo of an exam room, as if the visitor has already arrived at the clinic.

  1. Grooming next to veterinary care. Vet and grooming services stand side by side as equals, and the specialists are shown as one team. The clinic’s philosophy says it plainly: groomers know the specifics of each breed and work together with the vets, so every haircut is also a health check.

  1. “Compliments” instead of discounts. Special offers are called the clinic’s compliments: a special price and dates, but no percentages or crossed-out prices. Each compliment is a useful article. For example, about a digestion check-up: what to watch out for, what the program includes and how much it costs.

  1. Short booking. You can book through a service, through a vet, via a compliment, or just leave your number. I removed extra steps: after choosing a service there’s a short form right away, and people get in touch wherever it’s convenient — by phone or in a messenger.

  1. Result

Metrics are being collected: the landing page is live, and we’re now comparing it with the main website by the number of requests. The results will appear here as soon as they are ready.
