FoodBot

FoodBot is a service where cafés and restaurants launch their own Telegram bot for delivery orders — no programmers needed.

in this case

  1. Where does a café owner spend the day? Definitely not at a computer.

    Management moved into Telegram — 40% fewer missed orders.

  2. A bot with no programmer and no word “API”.

    5 clear steps instead of one scary field: 25% fewer people abandon the service.

  3. Support questions that were never asked.

    Hints right in the interface — 20% fewer requests.

  4. A tool that shows where users look — built with AI.

    The “I can do it myself with AI” section: what AI can and can’t do.

  1. Task

Build a dashboard that a café owner can figure out without technical knowledge. Setting up a bot should be as easy as filling in a social media profile.

  1. Solution

Research

  1. Who the users are. Owners of cafés, burger joints, sushi bars and coffee shops. They have little time, they’re always on their feet and often miss orders because they don’t see notifications.

  2. What competitors offer. Lots of features, but no hints. Owners figure things out by trial and error and spend hours on the first launch.

I fed competitor guides and reviews to AI, and within minutes it pulled out the main problems. That saved hours of manual analysis.

Hypothesis

Owners are scared by words like “API token” and confusing settings. Let’s do three things: turn the connection into a simple step-by-step guide, the menu into visual cards, and add hints next to unclear fields. Then bots will be launched faster and people will write to support less. And a mobile version right inside Telegram will help them stop missing orders.

What I did

  1. Simplified the connection. Instead of one intimidating field — 5 clear steps written in plain language.

AI rewrote Telegram’s dry technical guide in simple words; I only had to edit it.

  1. Designed screens for different situations. While there are no bots, the screen shows one big “Create a bot” button. Once bots appear, their cards and search come to the front.

With AI I built my own tool that shows where users look. It confirmed that attention goes where it should, and expensive A/B testing wasn’t needed.

  1. Added hints. Next to an unclear setting there is an icon: tap it and you immediately see an example. No need to leave the screen or write to support.

  1. Moved management into Telegram. The owner puts dishes on the stop list, changes prices and accepts orders right from their phone.

An AI agent assembled the draft Telegram screens from ready-made Telegram elements, which sped up the work several times over.

  1. Handed developers a clean file with a design system. New sections are added quickly and in a consistent style.

An AI agent in Figma prepared layer names, styles and comments for developers, saving hours.

  1. Result

25% fewer people abandon the service at the very start: it no longer looks like a complex database.

  • 30% faster first bot setup.

  • 20% fewer support requests: hints answer questions right in the interface.

  • 40% fewer missed orders: delivery management is now in their pocket.

  • 20% faster development of new sections.
